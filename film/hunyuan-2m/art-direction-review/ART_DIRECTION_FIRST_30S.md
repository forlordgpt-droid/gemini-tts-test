## Art direction

Hand-illustrated premium contemporary 2D animation. Cobalt/indigo pre-dawn, teal foliage and velvet curtains, amber practical lighting and peach blankets. In the later siren beats, inject magenta/cyan light and restricted signal red without muddying the scene. Rich architectural detail, naturally separated foreground/midground/background artwork, physically plausible contact. 16:9.

Display typography: **Space Grotesk Bold**, supporting interface: **Inter Medium/Regular**, with correctly licensed/font-embedded assets in the *animated* export. Text must be typeset cleanly in SVG/Canvas, not entrusted to generated-image lettering.

## Acceptance

Do not treat flat generated concept art as proof of real parallax, camera motion or animation. Before approval: actual full 30-second MP4, exact original Hunyuan 2D rig performing actions, independent multi-plane assets with clean patches and overscan, tested Three.js/GSAP camera paths, typography in exported canvas, visual inspection at 0/5/10/15/20/25/30s, 1920×1080 30fps with real narration. Do not substitute 3D characters or photorealistic scenes.
