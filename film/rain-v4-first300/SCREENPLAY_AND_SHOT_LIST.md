## Character-presence correction and quality gates

- The protagonist is deliberately staged throughout all applicable early-apartment, forecast, flood-watch, shop, packing, evacuation, and deadline scenes. No default scenery-only workaround.
- Walt appears **at 03:28**, never earlier. His visual introduction, reaction, cart contents, and fixed dialogue close-ups form a character scene, not an infographic.
- Nia first enters the original story at approximately **10:11**, outside this five-minute scope; do not insert her prematurely.
- The water-case standoff is represented through two fixed shopper depictions across cuts, not animated human tugging.
- Independent mechanisms (phone state, forecast paging, water rise, alert lamp, rain gusts, clock and transit deadline) must be visible in actual exported frames, with cause and consequences legible.
- Camera motions must differ by dramatic purpose; foreground, midground, protagonist, props and background occupy genuine depth planes, not flat repeating zooms.
- Preserve source phrasing and quantities (24th/27th floors, 30 stories, Tuesday, 10+10 rainy days, California 1861, 43 days, 10 feet, one cart and ten minutes, last bus 6 PM, 4:40 PM check).
- Verify every shot boundary, character screen-time target, object states, duration, PTS/DTS, audio intelligibility, shot composition and visual/voice cue correlation. A technical pass is not an artistic approval.
